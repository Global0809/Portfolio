Changes made for AICANFEEL:
Extracted full scan mesh from GLB; baked node scale; translated skull-to-chin midpoint to Y=0 and uniformly scaled crown-to-approximate-chin height to 4 units; rounded positions and preserved smooth normals to 5 decimal places; omitted materials, textures, UVs, cameras, and lights; original triangle winding and full bust retained.

Rendering derivatives, including a static fallback image, if present, use
this same geometry with artistic particles, lighting, and scan effects.

Geometry details:
9,279 vertices; 17,684 triangles. Normals preserve the smooth source GLB
normal values after inverse-transpose node-scale correction and normalization.
The Y=-0.9 source-space chin plane is an approximate visual presentation
landmark, not a measured biometric landmark. Crown Y=+2 and this plane Y=-2
in the output. The complete neck and shoulder bust extend below the plane
and may be faded in the display shader. +Y is up; +Z points forward.
